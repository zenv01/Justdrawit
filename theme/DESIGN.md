## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-spirited, tactile, and expressive multiplayer gaming atmosphere centered around **Playful Neo-Brutalism with Retro Pixel & Arcade Accents**. 

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Digital-native players, casual party-game enthusiasts, students, and competitive sketchers seeking fast-paced social entertainment.
- **Emotional Tone:** Electric, mischievous, tactile, and immediately comprehensible. The interface communicates energetic arcade nostalgia through bold solid outlines, offset punchy shadows, and pixelated title moments, balanced with pristine, ultra-legible modern controls that prevent cognitive fatigue during rapid drawing and guessing rounds.

### Design Movement & Core Philosophy
The visual direction fuses **Neo-brutalism** (unapologetic 3px–4px deep ink outlines, rigid unblurred drop shadows, and pill-shaped interactive chips) with **Classic 90s Arcade UI** (high-saturation badge alerts, chunky tactile buttons, playful avatar badges, and dotted matrix canvas staging). Every card feels like an arcade cart or physical toy module sitting atop an electric polka-dotted playfield.

## Colors

The palette balances a punchy chromatic backdrop against an ultra-clean neutral foreground, framing user creations without visual distortion:

- **Primary Action (`#22C55E`):** Vibrant Arcade Green serves as the universal cue for decisive progression—Start Game, Submit Guess, Ready Up, and Victory states.
- **Secondary Base (`#6B5BFF`):** Digital Purple anchors the macro viewport canvas. Overlaid with an engineered radial dot grid pattern, it sets a buoyant, modern gaming stage.
- **Tertiary & Gamification (`#FACC15`):** Retro Warning Gold drives urgency, active status markers, room codes, host crowns, and 1st-place podium ribbons.
- **Neutral Core (`#1E1E2F` & `#18181B`):** Deep Obsidian Ink forms structural 3px–4px containment borders and hard-edged 3D directional drop shadows.
- **Surface Foundations (`#F8F9FC` & `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity off-white provides anti-glare card surfaces, keeping toolbars, player slots, and drawing boards crisp and legible.
- **System Accents:** 
  - `timer-red` (`#EF4444`): High-alert countdown badge, eraser mode, and critical notifications.
  - `accent-blue` (`#3B82F6`): Collaborative status badges, active stroke modifiers, and informative tabs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs retro-futuristic geometric authority with warm, rounded character clarity:

- **Display & Structural Titles (`Space Grotesk`):** Built with bold geometric quirks and proportional cuts that evoke arcade leaderboards and 90s console menus. Applied in uppercase across game room codes, tab controls, primary actions, and category tags.
- **Conversational Content & Chat (`Rubik`):** Soft humanist grotesque letterforms provide rapid legibility across player handles, system tooltips, guess logs, and canvas challenge prompts in Thai and Latin alphabets.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing Strategy:** All labels, badges, and headers utilize positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.1em`) with uppercase transformation to simulate retro CRT terminal readouts. Body text and chat inputs retain neutral spacing with default casing for effortless parsing.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Architecture
The layout centers on a framed, modular cabinet model. The outer canvas hosts an infinite dot grid matrix (`background-size: 24px 24px`), while application screens reside inside a centered, structural card module maxing out at `1120px` width on desktop and scaling fluidly across viewport widths.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Asymmetrical split-panel layouts (65% primary stage / 35% sidekick roster, chat panel, or settings pane). Inner gutters locked at `1.25rem` (20px), outer safe margins set to `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Compact dual-card layout; toolbar collapses into an ergonomic docked dock below the canvas.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked deck. Sidebar panels morph into slide-up bottom sheets or tabbed sub-views with minimum 44px tap targets and `1rem` outer margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally bypasses soft, feathered Gaussian blurs in favor of **Neo-Brutalist Hard-Offset Drop Shadows**:

1. **Elevation 0 (Flat Plaque):**
   - Stroke: 3px solid `#1E1E2F`
   - Shadow: None
   - Used for inner drawing boards, inset text input fields, and recessed container slots.

2. **Elevation 1 (Interactive Element / Chip / Button Default):**
   - Stroke: 3px solid `#1E1E2F`
   - Shadow: `3px 3px 0px 0px #18181B`
   - Hover: `4px 4px 0px 0px #18181B`, translate `(-1px, -1px)`
   - Active: `0px 0px 0px 0px #18181B`, translate `(3px, 3px)`

3. **Elevation 2 (Primary Stage / Floating Panels / Modals):**
   - Stroke: 4px solid `#1E1E2F`
   - Shadow: `6px 6px 0px 0px #18181B`
   - Mobile: Scaled to `4px 4px 0px 0px #18181B`

4. **Elevation Inset (Recessed Trays & Empty Slots):**
   - Stroke: 2px dashed `#A0AEC0` or 3px solid `#1E1E2F`
   - Background: `#EEF1F8` with inner shadow `inset 2px 2px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The design uses **Rounded Geometric Neo-brutalism** (`roundedness: 2`). While strict brutalism relies on razor-sharp corners, this playful arcade variant softens outer silhouettes to maintain an inviting, toy-like tactile feel:

- **Macro Cards & App Shells:** `rounded-2xl` (20px to 24px) paired with 4px dark borders.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Modals:** `rounded-xl` (12px to 14px) with 3px dark borders.
- **Chips, Badges, and Tooltips:** Full pill contours (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular panels.
- **Player Avatar Tokens:** Perfectly circular badges encased in a 3px dark border with a solid bottom-right shadow crescent.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Style:** Pill-shaped or rounded-xl (12px), bordered with 3px `#1E1E2F`.
- **Primary Action (Start / Submit):** Solid `#22C55E` fill, white bold uppercase `Space Grotesk` text, shadow `4px 4px 0px #18181B`. On hover, shifts `-1px, -1px` with shadow `5px 5px 0px #18181B`. On active click, depresses `+4px, +4px` with zero shadow.
- **Secondary (Back / Settings):** Neutral off-white `#F8F9FC` or `#EEF1F8` fill, `#1E1E2F` typography, identical tactile click mechanics.
- **Danger (Clear / Leave):** `#EF4444` fill with crisp white text.

### 2. Room Lobby & Code Display Card
- **Header Badge:** Pill-shaped badge filled with `#FACC15`, 3px `#1E1E2F` border, containing `ROOM ID: [CODE]` and a solid-bordered clipboard icon button.
- **Player Roster Module:** Off-white card frame housing vertically stacked player badges.
- **Player Slot (Filled):** Rounded-full container (`#F8F9FC`), 3px `#1E1E2F` border, circular avatar badge on the left, player name in bold `Rubik`, and host crown icon (`👑`) on the right.
- **Player Slot (Empty):** Rounded-full container with 2px dashed `#CBD5E1` border, light gray text reading `"WAITING FOR PLAYER..."`.

### 3. Canvas & Drawing Workbench
- **Whiteboard Area:** High-visibility `#FFFFFF` canvas panel framed by a 4px `#1E1E2F` contour with a subtle 4px corner radius.
- **Top Game Status Bar:** Inset status row featuring:
  - Drawer indicator: Avatar icon + `"[Player] is drawing [Keyword]"`
  - Synchronized Timer Pill: `#EF4444` background, white monospaced bold text, pulsating on critical remaining time (< 10 seconds).
- **Drawing Dock (Bottom Bar):** Horizontal tool cluster framed with 3px `#1E1E2F` border:
  - Color Swatches: 32px circular buttons with 2px solid dark borders. Active color displays a prominent highlight ring.
  - Brush Slider: Track styled with 2px `#1E1E2F` border, `#22C55E` fill, and a chunky circular thumb.
  - Live Shape Draft Indicator: Semi-transparent dashed bounding box preview for geometric shapes.

### 4. Input Fields & Chat Guessing Box
- **Text Inputs:** 3px `#1E1E2F` border, `#FFFFFF` surface, `14px` border radius, uppercase placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Border color shifts to `#6B5BFF` with an immediate `3px 3px 0px 0px #6B5BFF` shadow offset.
- **Chat Feed:** Alternating chat speech pills with instant colored state cues:
  - Normal guess: Neutral off-white pill.
  - Close guess ("You're close!"): `#FEF08A` yellow alert pill.
  - Correct guess: `#DCFCE7` vibrant green celebration pill with concealed keyword masking.

### 5. Leaderboard & Podium Display
- **Top 3 Podium Cards:** Staggered height blocks (1st place elevated in center with `#FACC15` crown banner, 2nd place `#E2E8F0`, 3rd place `#FDBA74`).
- **Scoreboard Rows:** Tabular striped cards with 3px solid borders, player avatar thumbnail, point totals rendered in bold `Space Grotesk`, and positive increment animations (`+150 PTS`) flying upward.
